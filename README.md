### Hi, I'm Abhisek

I study how reinforcement learning agents generalize to unseen environments — and whether the evaluation protocols the field relies on actually measure what they claim to.

**Currently:** Senior Data Scientist at [Target](https://www.target.com/) · Independent RL researcher

**Research focus:** Generalization in procedurally generated RL environments, with emphasis on evaluation methodology, convergence diagnostics, and architectural inductive biases.

**Background:** B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering (CS Minor) from [IIT Jammu](https://iitjammu.ac.in/) · Director's Gold Medal · RL foundations through independent study (Ravindran, Brunskill) and the ISI Kolkata Winter Schools on Deep Learning (2023, 2025)
